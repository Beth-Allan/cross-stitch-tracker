## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Keyboard-gated autocomplete highlight — hasUsedArrowKeys flag prevents highlight on type/mouse
- FocalPointEditor split into overlay + action bar — full image clickable for placement
- Synchronous localStorage in useState initializer — eliminates view mode flash
- aria-labelledby without role override on tr — preserves implicit row role for table semantics

**Issues Resolved:**
- Supply actions now reject operations for wrong-user projects (CRIT-01)

- Stats cache invalidated on chart status + supply mutations (RELY-02, RELY-03)
- 126 vacuous assertions replaced with assertSuccess guards (QUAL-16)
- 47 WHAT-comments and 20 JSX section markers removed (QUAL-06, QUAL-07)
