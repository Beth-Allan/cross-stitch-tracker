# Sketch 003: Wizard Flow & Layout

## Design Question
How should the add-chart experience flow as a single continuous page with progressive depth? The current 8-section form is a wall of fields. This sketch consolidates into 3 natural groups — "The Chart" (what is it?), "The Project" (where in your workflow?), "Supplies" (what does it need?) — and explores how to make save-anywhere feel natural.

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/003-wizard-flow/index.html

## Variants
- **A: Section Cards** — All three sections visible as stacked cards with numbered headers. Sticky bottom bar with save. Everything scrollable from the start — the most direct "intake form" feel.
- **B: Progressive Reveal** — Chart basics always visible. Green milestone markers between sections celebrate progress ("Chart basics complete!") and invite you deeper ("Add project details →"). Sections expand/collapse. The guided-but-not-forced feel.
- **C: Sidebar Nav** — Sticky left sidebar with section links, completion dots (empty/partial/complete), and field counts (2/8). Save button lives in the sidebar. On mobile, sidebar becomes a horizontal breadcrumb bar. The docs-TOC feel.

## What to Look For
- **Does the 3-section grouping feel right?** Chart / Project / Supplies vs. the current 8 sections.
- **Save-anywhere:** Which variant makes "I can stop here" feel most natural?
- **Section transitions:** Does the form feel like one continuous experience or three disconnected blocks?
- **Supply integration:** Does the supply table feel like part of the same form or a jarring mode switch?
- **Information density:** Too much? Too sparse? Right amount of breathing room?
- **Mobile (try 📱 button):** Which degrades best at 375px?
