# Stitching sessions and progress

How Beth's stitching time is counted: which date a session belongs to, where a week starts, and
what a project's progress is measured against. The stats themselves are the app's; this file holds
the rules they rest on. The words (WIP, BAP) are in `vocabulary.md`.

Opened 2026-10-25 from Beth's answers to three questions the 2026-10-24 `/cleanup` routed to
`/stitch-fact` (maintenance-ledger rows of 2026-08-17 and 2026-08-19).

---

### SES-001 — A stitching week starts on Monday

Monday to Sunday. Every "this week" figure and every weekly display uses the same week. Chosen over
Sunday to Saturday.

Today the stats page's "this week" counts from Sunday while the calendar and the day-of-week chart
are Monday-first, so on a Sunday they disagree. (The question put to her said "the dashboard"; the
figure is on the stats page. Her answer does not depend on where it is shown.) Fixed by item F-17.

[stated by Beth 2026-10-25]

### SES-002 — A session belongs to the date where Beth is when she stitches

When she travels, a session counts under the local date where she stitched it, not under the app's
home calendar (Edmonton). Chosen over "always Edmonton's date", on the reasoning that a session
belongs to the day she actually stitched it.

Today the logging box offers her device's date, but the server refuses anything later than
Edmonton's today, so somewhere ahead of Edmonton it can refuse a date the box itself offered.
Fixed by item F-17.

[stated by Beth 2026-10-25]

### SES-003 — With no stitch count entered, a chart's total is width × height

When a chart has its width and height but no stitch count, **width × height is its total
stitches** wherever progress is measured. She chose it knowing it counts every square in the
design's rectangle, unstitched ones included, so for a design with open fabric the total is too
big and the percentage reads low. A low percentage was preferred to a flat 0%, and to showing no
percentage until a count is entered.

Today the gallery card, the project hero and the catalogue rows already use it, while the
dashboards, the session picker and the completion estimates use the raw count and read 0%. Fixed by
item F-17.

[stated by Beth 2026-10-25]
