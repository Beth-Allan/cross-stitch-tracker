# Beads — Mill Hill's types and numbers

How the beads Beth stitches with are named and numbered: the types a maker sorts them into, and
which numbers are the same bead. Thread is in `threads.md`; Mill Hill is named there as a bead
maker (THR-003).

Opened 2026-10-29 from Beth's answers at the `/stitch-fact` that preceded Stage C's catalogues
(`build-plan.md` §Stage C, D-42 ④ — the whole Mill Hill sheet).

---

### BEAD-001 — Mill Hill's twelve bead types, by the sheet's names

**Glass Seed, Antique Seed, Crayon Seed, Frosted Seed, Petite Seed, Pebble, Size 6, Size 8, Small
Bugle, Medium Bugle, Large Bugle, Magnifica.** Put to her as the twelve types on Wichelt's official
Mill Hill bead sheet, shown beside each bead in the app; she answered "Yes, those names".

_What was put to her was the plan's transcription of the sheet's headings, not the sheet itself
(`build-plan.md` §Stage C sources table). If a heading on the sheet reads differently from this
list, that is a stop-and-ask for C-2, not a silent pick of either spelling._

[stated by Beth 2026-10-29]

### BEAD-002 — A pack size is not a different bead

Mill Hill sells some beads in a 1,000-bead pack numbered the same with a 2 at the front instead of
a 0, and Magnifica in a 7-gram pack numbered with 11 instead of 10. Each is the same bead as its
regular number, so the app keeps **one entry per bead, not one per pack size**. Put to her as the
sheet's rule; she chose "Same bead, one entry".

[stated by Beth 2026-10-29]
