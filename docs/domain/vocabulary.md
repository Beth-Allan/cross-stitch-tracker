# Vocabulary — the words themselves

The cross-stitch terms this app's data model, UI and conversation use. Getting one of these
wrong shows up everywhere at once, which is why they are facts rather than style.

Seeded 2026-08-16 (overhaul step 5) from `CROSS_STITCH_TRACKER_PLAN.md` §3, the glossary Beth
wrote her requirements into. Nothing here has been said back to her since; `/stitch-fact` is how
each one upgrades to her word.

---

### VOC-001 — Chart vs. project

A **chart** (or **pattern**) is the _design_ — the instructions. A **project** is the _instance
of working on it_. A chart can be digital (PDF), paper, or part of a kit. The two words are used
somewhat interchangeably in the early stages of acquiring something, but the distinction is real
and the data model keeps it.

### VOC-003 — FFO

**F**ully **F**inished **O**bject — a project that has been stitched **and** finished: framed,
made into a pillow, or otherwise turned into the final thing. **Distinct from "finished
stitching"** — a piece can be off the frame and not yet an FFO, and the app tracks the two
states separately.

[from the project plan 2026-08-16 — CROSS_STITCH_TRACKER_PLAN.md §3, not re-confirmed]

### VOC-004 — SAL

**S**titch-**A**long — a single design released in parts over time, typically monthly. One
project, multiple chart parts arriving progressively. The plan's wording: stitch count and supply
needs **may** evolve as new parts are released — so a SAL's totals are not necessarily fixed at
the point of kitting. Whether they always move, and what that should mean for a SAL already
kitted, is Beth's to say and is not recorded here.

[from the project plan 2026-08-16 — CROSS_STITCH_TRACKER_PLAN.md §3, not re-confirmed]

### VOC-005 — Series

A collection of **independent but related** patterns — "Mini Bottles", "Celtic Santas". Each
chart is its own project; they belong to a named group. The plan likens it to a book-series
tracker.

[from the project plan 2026-08-16 — CROSS_STITCH_TRACKER_PLAN.md §3, not re-confirmed]

### VOC-006 — BAP

**B**ig **A**ss **P**roject — the community's term for a very large design. The plan's figure is
**50,000+ stitches**.

### VOC-007 — Stash

The stitcher's collection of supplies — thread, fabric, beads, charts. "Checking stash" before
buying is a step in kitting (see `kitting-and-storage.md`).

[from the project plan 2026-08-16 — CROSS_STITCH_TRACKER_PLAN.md §3, not re-confirmed]

### VOC-008 — Onion skinning

Re-charting a paper chart, or a PDF that lacks the needed detail, in charting software so it can
be loaded properly into an iPad stitching app. Needed particularly where a design has heavy
backstitch. It is a real unit of work that happens _before_ a chart is stitchable, which is why
it is one of the nine kitting conditions (KIT-001).

[from the project plan 2026-08-16 — CROSS_STITCH_TRACKER_PLAN.md §3, not re-confirmed]

### VOC-009 — Digital working copy

A personal-use digital copy of a chart (PDF or image), stored in the app for reference.
**Permitted as long as it is never shared.**
